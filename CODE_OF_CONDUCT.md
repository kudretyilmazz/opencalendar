# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a GitHub security advisory or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this code.
